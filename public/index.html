    How to play Lantern Keepers

    The story. You and your friends are keepers of the Last Lantern, the only light holding back a spreading dark. A Night is made of five Watches (rounds). The flame drops a little each Watch. Keep it burning until dawn.

    The twist. One or two of you are secretly Shadows — touched by the dark, working to snuff the flame out without being caught. Everyone else is a Keeper.

    Each Watch:

    1. The Riddle. A hidden category appears. Keepers see it clearly. Shadows only see a vague hint — they must bluff.
    2. Sparks. Everyone secretly types one word that fits the category (a "spark"). Sparks are hidden until the reveal.
    3. The Reveal. All sparks appear together, anonymously and shuffled.
    4. Lighting. You have 2 lights. Place them on sparks you think are genuine — lighting a real spark brightens the flame. But lighting a Shadow's dark spark dims it.
    5. Suspicion. Cast one suspicion on the spark you think the Shadow wrote. If enough of you agree and you're right, that Shadow is exposed and the flame surges. Accuse a true Keeper and the flame dips.

    Winning. If the flame survives all five Watches, dawn breaks — Keepers win. If it hits zero, the dark wins and Shadows triumph. The top scorer is crowned Best Keeper either way.

    2 players: a friendly cooperative watch — no Shadows, just keep the flame lit together.
